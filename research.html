Research

Fluid equations and kinetic theory

I study incompressible fluid equations and vortex dynamics. My recent work also extends to kinetic equations, with a particular interest in Landau damping for Vlasov–Poisson systems.

Current themes

  • Incompressible fluidsRegularity, singularity formation, and long-time dynamics.
  • Vortex patch dynamicsBoundary evolution, rotating structures, and multiple connectivity.
  • Kinetic equationsLandau damping and dispersive behavior for Vlasov–Poisson systems.
